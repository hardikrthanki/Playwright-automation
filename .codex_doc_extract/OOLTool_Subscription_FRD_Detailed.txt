OOLTool Subscription Management Journey
Functional Requirement Document (FRD)
Version 1.0 - Client Review
Introduction
This document defines the subscription lifecycle, billing behaviour, trial experience,
plan upgrades, downgrades, billing frequency changes, cancellations, refunds and payment failure handling.
Global Business Rules
• Trial available once per lifetime.
• Trial duration: 30 days.
• All subscriptions auto-renew by default.
• Upgrades are immediate and use Stripe proration.
• Downgrades are effective on next renewal date.
• Monthly→Annual is immediate.
• Annual→Monthly is effective on next renewal date.
• Refunds are manual and admin approved only.
• Trial expiry deletes portfolio data immediately.
• Cancellation expiry deletes portfolio data immediately.
• Failed payment expiry deletes portfolio data immediately.
• Email notifications are mandatory for all key subscription events.
• One-time retention offer may be shown during downgrade/cancellation journeys.

Existing paid subscribers are not eligible for the trial.

Subscription Journey – Use Case 4
Downgrade Subscription
Objective
Allow users to downgrade from a higher subscription plan to a lower subscription plan while ensuring transparent communication regarding feature limitations, data loss, retention offers, and the effective downgrade date.
Eligible Downgrade Paths
Examples:
Marketplace → Portfolio Hedger
Portfolio Hedger → Overlay Strategists
Overlay Strategists → Income Builder
Marketplace → Income Builder
Users may request a downgrade at any time during an active subscription.
Monthly Subscription Retention Offer
Before processing a monthly subscription downgrade, the system must present a retention offer to encourage the user to remain on their current plan.
Retention Offer Rules
Applicable only for Monthly-to-Monthly downgrades.
The user is offered a discount on their current subscription plan for the next 3 billing cycles.
The retention offer can be used only once per account lifetime.
If the user accepts the offer, the downgrade request is canceled and the current subscription continues with the discounted pricing for the next 3 months.
If the user declines the offer, the downgrade process continues.
The system must track whether the retention offer has already been used by the account.
Example
Current Subscription
Portfolio Hedger Monthly
Price: $149/month
Retention Offer
20% discount for the next 3 months (example percentage)
Discount applies to the current plan only
Result if Accepted
Downgrade request is canceled.
User remains on Portfolio Hedger.
Discount is applied for the next 3 monthly billing cycles.
Retention offer is marked as used and cannot be offered again for the account.
Downgrade Request
The user navigates to:
Subscription Settings
Billing & Subscription Page
The system displays:
Current Plan
New Plan
Effective Downgrade Date
Features That Will Be Lost
Account Limits of New Plan
Data Impact Warning
For eligible monthly subscriptions, the retention offer must be displayed before the downgrade confirmation step.
The user must confirm the downgrade request before it is scheduled.
Billing Logic
The platform follows a customer-friendly and dispute-free downgrade policy.
When a downgrade is requested:
No refund is issued.
No credit is issued.
No wallet balance is created.
Current subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.
Downgrade becomes effective on the next renewal date.
For monthly subscriptions:
The retention offer is presented before scheduling the downgrade if it has not previously been used by the account.
If the retention offer is accepted, no downgrade is scheduled.

Objective

Subscription Settings

Impact of Cancellation
The user must confirm the cancellation request.
Cancellation Options by Billing Frequency
Monthly Subscription
For monthly subscriptions, users can only cancel at the end of the current billing period.
When the user cancels:
Auto-renewal is disabled immediately.
No future recurring charges will be processed.
Subscription remains active until the current billing period ends.
No refund is issued.
Access continues until the subscription expiry date.
Yearly Subscription
For yearly subscriptions, users are provided with two cancellation options:
Option 1: Cancel at Expiry
When selected:
Auto-renewal is disabled immediately.
Subscription remains active until the annual subscription expiry date.
No refund is issued.
No future recurring charges will be processed.
Option 2: Cancel and Refund
When selected:
Subscription is canceled immediately.
User receives a refund for the unused remaining months of the annual subscription.
Refund amount is calculated based on the remaining subscription period.
Access to paid features is removed once the cancellation and refund are processed.
User is moved to the Free Plan.
Billing Logic
Monthly Subscription
When a monthly subscription is canceled:
Auto-renewal is disabled immediately.
No future recurring charges will be processed.
Current subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.
No refund is issued.
Yearly Subscription – Cancel at Expiry
When a yearly subscription is canceled at expiry:
Auto-renewal is disabled immediately.
No future recurring charges will be processed.
Current subscription remains active until the annual expiry date.
No refund is issued.
Yearly Subscription – Cancel and Refund
When a yearly subscription is canceled with a refund request:
Subscription is terminated immediately after refund approval and processing.
Refund is calculated based on the unused remaining months.
User loses access to paid features after cancellation is completed.
User is moved to the Free Plan.

Initial Payment Failure
When Stripe attempts to process a subscription renewal and payment fails:
System Actions
Subscription remains active.
User retains access to all paid features.
Payment failure is logged.
Email notification is sent.
In-app notification is displayed.
User is prompted to update their payment method.
Retry Schedule
The platform will automatically retry payment collection.
Retry Attempts
Attempt
Schedule
Initial Attempt
Renewal Date
Retry 1

The retry schedule may be adjusted based on Stripe billing configuration.
Grace Period
During the retry period:
Subscription remains active.
User retains access to all premium features.
User may update their payment method at any time.
Outstanding invoices remain payable.

Final Outcome
If all retry attempts fail:
Subscription is marked as Expired.
User is automatically moved to the Curious Explorer (Free) plan.
Premium features are disabled.
Portfolio data removed and send email notification to user
Subscription Recovery
The user may recover the subscription at any time during the retention period.
Recovery Process
User:
Updates payment method.
Pays outstanding invoice.
Reactivates subscription.
Result
Subscription becomes active.
Premium features are restored.
Existing portfolio data remains available.
Portfolio Retention Policy
When a subscription expires due to payment failure:
Day 0
User is moved to the Free Plan.
Portfolio data is retained.
Day 3
Final reminder email sent.
Portfolio deletion warning displayed.
Day 7
Portfolio positions and portfolio-related data are permanently deleted.
Broker integrations are disconnected.
Portfolio recovery is no longer possible.

Important Business Rules
Users retain premium access during the payment retry period.
Multiple payment retry attempts must be performed automatically.
Email notifications are mandatory at every payment failure stage.
Users are automatically moved to the Free Plan after final payment failure.
Portfolio data is retained for 30 days after subscription expiry.
Users may reactivate their subscription during the retention period.
Portfolio data is permanently deleted after the retention period expires.
Broker integrations are disconnected when portfolio data is deleted.
All payment and recovery events must be logged for audit purposes.
Administrators may extend the retention period in exceptional cases.
