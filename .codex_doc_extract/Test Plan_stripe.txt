Test Plan – OOLTool Subscription Management Journey
Document Version: 1.0
Source: Subscription Management Journey FRD – Client Review
Module: Subscription, Billing, Stripe, Entitlements, Portfolio Retention, Notifications, and Admin Management
Test Type: Functional, Integration, Database, API/Webhooks, Negative, Regression, Audit, and Data-Lifecycle Validation
1. Test Objective
Validate that the complete subscription lifecycle works correctly for all supported plans and billing frequencies, including:
Overlay Strategists trial with and without a saved card
Direct subscription purchase
Automatic renewal
Plan upgrade and Stripe proration
Plan downgrade and scheduled downgrade activation
Monthly-to-Annual and Annual-to-Monthly billing changes
Subscription cancellation and cancellation reversal
Annual refund processing
Failed payment retries, grace period, recovery, and expiry
Portfolio data retention and deletion
Email and in-app notifications
Stripe, database, audit-log, and admin-management behaviour
The primary QA goal is to ensure users receive the correct plan access, billing outcome, notifications, and data treatment without incorrect charges, premature data loss, duplicate subscriptions, or missing audit records.

3. Critical Requirement Clarifications Required Before Execution
The following items conflict or remain incomplete in the FRD. These should be confirmed by the business team before destructive test execution.
ID
Requirement Conflict / Gap
Impact
Required Decision
CL-01
Failed-payment expiry is stated as immediate portfolio deletion in Global Business Rules, deletion on Day 7 in the Failed Payment journey, and retention for 30 days in the Important Business Rules.
High risk of incorrect data deletion.
Confirm the exact retention duration: immediate, 7 days, or 30 days.
CL-02
Global rules state refunds are manual and admin-approved only, while annual cancellation describes a user selecting “Cancel and Refund.”
Refunds could be processed without approval.
Confirm whether the user submits a refund request or receives an automatic refund.
CL-03
A one-time retention offer may be shown during downgrade and cancellation journeys, but detailed rules describe it only for monthly downgrades.
Inconsistent customer journey.
Confirm whether retention offers apply to cancellation flows.
CL-04
Trial Option B failed payment mentions a configurable grace period, but no duration or retry schedule is finalized.
Trial conversion failure cannot be validated accurately.
Confirm grace period and retry schedule for first paid charge after trial.
CL-05
Annual refund calculation is based on “remaining unused months.” Partial months, taxes, discounts, credits, and Stripe fees are not defined.
Incorrect refund amount.
Confirm refund formula and rounding rule.
CL-06
Reminder emails are mandatory, but email trigger time zone, retry handling, and duplicate prevention are not defined.
Duplicate or missed reminders.
Confirm scheduler time zone and idempotency rules.
CL-07
Trial eligibility includes “other identifiable account information,” but identifiers are not defined.
Trial-abuse control cannot be fully tested.
Confirm supported identifiers: email, card fingerprint, device, account ID, phone, IP, etc.
CL-08
“Portfolio-related data” is not precisely defined.
Some related records may remain after deletion.
Confirm whether positions, option positions, broker links, portfolios, uploaded files, reports, and analytics history must be deleted.
QA Recommendation: Do not execute irreversible portfolio-deletion cases in a shared environment until CL-01 and CL-08 are approved.

9. Negative and Edge Case Coverage

Negative / Edge Scenario

Data Deletion

Security
User tries to modify another user’s subscription ID, refund endpoint without admin role, replayed Stripe webhook

11. Entry Criteria
Testing can begin when:
Subscription plans and Stripe price IDs are configured in UAT.
Stripe Test Mode access is available.
Test users and admin user are available.
Trial, renewal, retry, and deletion jobs can be triggered or time-shifted.
Email-capture method is available.
Database/API access is available for validation.
Critical requirement clarifications are approved, especially payment-failure retention and refund approval flow.
Free-plan limits and plan-specific limits are documented.
12. Exit Criteria
Testing can be closed when:
All P0 scenarios are executed.
No open Critical or High severity defect remains.
Subscription, payment, Stripe, entitlement, and database records are consistent.
No duplicate charges, duplicate subscriptions, or duplicate notifications are found.
Data deletion is validated only against approved retention rules.
Audit logs are available for all major lifecycle actions.
Required emails and in-app notifications are delivered successfully.
Regression testing is completed after fixes.
